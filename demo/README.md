# Demonstration

The repository is ready for a short demonstration recording named `agrinox-demo.webm` in this directory.

Recommended review flow:

1. Sign in and open the bilingual system dashboard.
2. Open **My Farm**, select a farm and review its fields on the map.
3. Open **Device Management**, show automatic online/offline status and sensor readings.
4. Open **Weather Intelligence**, **Soil Quality & Advisory**, and **Smart Irrigation** for the same field.
5. Ask a bilingual question in **Agricultural Knowledge** and inspect its verified sources.
6. Demonstrate **Crop Planning**, calendar linking, Plant Lens and farm financial planning.

The video itself is not committed until a final recording is available, keeping the initial repository small and reviewable.
